# D2C Customer Retention & Loyalty Strategy Analysis

## Business Problem
D2C brands often use discounts to drive short-term sales, but excessive promotion can create **discount-dependent customers and margin pressure**.
This project analyzes customer transaction data to identify **high-value customers, loyalty patterns, promotional dependency, and retention opportunities**—turning customer analytics into an actionable growth strategy.

## Data & Tools
* **Data:** D2C fashion customer transaction data
* **SQL:** Customer segmentation, aggregation, promotional dependency analysis
* **Python:** Data cleaning, feature engineering & behavioral analysis
* **Power BI:** Executive customer analytics dashboard

## Approach
1. Built customer-level features including spend, AOV, frequency, discount rate, promotional ratio, and ratings.
2. Segmented customers by **revenue value** and **promotion dependency**.
3. Compared loyal/high-value customers with discount-dependent customers.
4. Identified behavioral and commercial opportunities.
5. Converted findings into a **Retention Playbook** and Founder Dashboard.

## Key Findings
* **545 Very High Revenue customers** showed **no promo dependency**, with average spend of **91.15**.
* Very High Revenue customers averaged **91.09** spend vs. **29.52** for Low Revenue customers.
* High promo-dependency customers accounted for **99,411** in discounted spend.
* High customer value does **not necessarily require continuous discounting**.

## Business Recommendations
* Reduce blanket discounting and use **targeted promotions**.
* Build loyalty programs around **high-value, low-promo-dependent customers**.
* Allocate retention resources according to **customer value and behavioral potential**.
* Use discounts as targeted behavioral interventions rather than default incentives.
